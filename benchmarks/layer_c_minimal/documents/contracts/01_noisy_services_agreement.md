# Regional Services Agreement (Noise-Realistic Draft)

## Payment Timing
1 The Customer must pay all undisputed invoices within thirty days after receipt.
2 Notwithstanding the thirty day payment timing in clause 1, Customer may withhold disputed invoice amounts for defective Deliverables until Seller cures the defect.
3 Order Form Schedule A states that Customer must pay all invoices within fifteen days after receipt.
4 Buyer payment of invoices under Schedule A conflicts with Agreement payment of invoices and Schedule A shall control for timing only.

## Termination And Cure
5 Either party may terminate for material breach after the breaching party fails to cure within ten days after written notice.
6 When Customer terminates under clause 5, Seller shall refund prepaid unused fees within thirty days.
7 Subject to clause 5, Customer must continue paying undisputed fees during any cure period.

## Escalation Notes (messy operational addendum)
8 After repeated payment disputes continue across two invoice cycles, escalate to the commercial operations lead before legal holds are issued.
9 Unless a temporary commercial waiver is approved in writing, the Schedule A fifteen day timing remains in force.
