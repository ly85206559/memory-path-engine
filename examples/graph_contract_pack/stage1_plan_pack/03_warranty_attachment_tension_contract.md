# Warranty Attachment Tension Contract

## Master Agreement
1 Seller warrants goods for twelve months from delivery.
2 Buyer must notify Seller of any warranty claim within thirty days of discovery.
3 Unless the Attachment states otherwise, Seller warrants goods for twelve months from delivery.
4 Buyer payment of invoices under this Agreement conflicts with Attachment payment of invoices.

## Attachment Schedule
5 Notwithstanding Section 1, Seller warrants goods for six months from delivery for refurbished units.
6 If refurbished units fail within the shortened warranty, Seller must replace or refund within ten days.
7 Buyer must pay all Attachment invoices within ten days of receipt.
8 Buyer payment of invoices under this Attachment conflicts with Agreement payment of invoices and shall control.
9 Except when Buyer fails to notify Seller within thirty days, Seller must honor the Attachment warranty remedy.
